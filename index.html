How it works

Three simple steps to join the network

1

Join the network

Cafés sign up, set their reward, and get a unique QR code. Customers to scan — no app download needed.

2

Earn anywhere

Buy coffee at any participating café. Stamps accumulate in one shared wallet, no matter where you earn them.

3

Redeem anywhere

Hit 10 stamps? Choose any café in the network for your free coffee. We handle the settlement automatically.

Automatic settlement

When a customer redeems at a different café, we calculate exactly who owes what

Built for independents

Everything you need to run a shared loyalty network

No app downloads

Customers use their phone browser. No friction, no app store, no updates.

Cross-café settlement

We track every stamp origin and automatically calculate who owes what when rewards are redeemed.

Fraud protection

Staff authentication, rate limiting, and immutable audit logs prevent fake stamps and duplicate rewards.

Financial ledger

Every transaction is recorded immutably. Corrections create reversal entries, never deletions.

Multilingual ready

Built for English now, with architecture ready for Traditional Chinese and Simplified Chinese.

Works on any device

Customers use their phone. Café owners use phone, tablet, or laptop. No special hardware needed.
